About

I am a postdoctoral researcher at INSA Lyon (CITI laboratory, Inria Agora), working on network function placement in satellite communications within the DONUTS project (PEPR Networks of the Future).

I received my PhD in Computer Science from INSA Lyon in July 2026, with a thesis on efficient downlink communication for LoRaWAN, advised by Dr. Oana Iova and Prof. Fabrice Valois. Before that, I studied electronic engineering at Universidad Técnica Federico Santa María (Chile), where I worked on memristors and in-memory computing.
